1:10 RC Design Project

2-3 years ago during my gap year I started designing this RC car. In the photos below you can see the first, second and third major iterations in descending order, each of which were done in one burst, year after year. It started out quite rudimentary, the suspension was done... not well, leading to frequent crashes and breaks. Additionally, little attention was paid towards exploiting the anisotropic nature of 3D printed parts. I was very new to 3D printing at this point and it was done before my second year of Uni so I hadn't been blessed with D&P or VD yet.

With the second iteration, done after the second year, major improvements can be seen in both the suspension and chassis structure, better exploiting the anisotropy. Effectively all elements were printed flat without supports. I also reverse engineered the traxxas gearbox to make my own housing to convert it to mid engine, improving the weight distribution and giving me absolute freedom with the hardpoints. It was much improved, lighter, stronger, faster to print, better handling. But then I crashed it and had another year hiatus.

And now, I've been working on V3. In this version I redid the front and rear suspension. Prior to this I was clamping down onto joints, causing alot of stiction in the suspension. This stiction would cause the suspension to bind under low loads, making it insensitive and unstable. With this knowledge, i redesigned it so that there is no clamping force on any of the joints, allowing the suspension to pivot with no effort. Then with the front end, I was dissatisfied with both my steering and suspension system. The links had alot of slop because of the way they were designed and the steering was also sloppy, all of which leads to a less predictable suspension. In the redesign I used Lancaster Links/swingarms. These are not ideal kinematically but they are rigid, simple, easy to print, and strong. I also flipped the servo 90 degrees and added another steering bar linkage, improving the steering rigidity.

Finally I redid the wing. I only realize after I did the aero module that my wing sucks and would just stall. Now it uses a NACA 6412 profile at a 10 deg angle which is hopefully more effective.

All in all it drives great now, have been thrashing it for the past 2 days but havent broken it yet. Usually these tests end because I break something, but the past tests have all ended because I had enough for one day.

Thats all up until now. What I like is that you can clearly see my skill and knowledge develop as I learned more at University.

Thanks for reading :)
