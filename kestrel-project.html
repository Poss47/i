Riley Kestrel Mesh Insert

Problem
At OUMF, we have a Riley Kestrel 1100. This car has a critical design flaw causing premature deterioration via leaves irrevocably entering the cowl vent. There is no way to access the inside of the cowl vent. OEM and aftermarket solutions exist that cover the vent, but don’t look aesthetically pleasing, to the extent that some owners would rather not use them despite the severity of the issue.

Solution
I mapped the geometry of the vent in CAD and designed this 3d printable mesh insert that slides under the slots rather than over, and secures itself up into the slots. At the Birmingham NEC, the chairman of the 1100 club was asked if he noticed anything different with the front of the car. He was unable to spot it even when guided to the bonnet, proving it was hidden succesfully.

This may seem like an obvious solution to the problem having seen it, but dont let its simplicity undermine the challenge. Remember, Leyland and aftermarket engineers also attempted the problem and were not able to produce an elegant solution. I enjoy finding the most simple and elegant solutions to problems and this is one of those cases. For mass production manufacture this could be made with stamped sheet metal with the ends rolled to form the tab
