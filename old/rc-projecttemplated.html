1:10 Scale RC Car Chassis & Drivetrain

Full CAD modeling, bespoke transmission engineering, and physical manufacturing of a custom 1:10 scale remote-controlled car chassis with tailored suspension geometry and aerodynamic testing.

Scale

1:10

Primary Tools

SolidWorks / Fusion 360

Manufacturing

3D Printing (FDM) & Machining

Testing Method

Iterative Physical & Aero

Engineering Overview

  • Chassis & Packaging Architecture: Designed a complete ground-up 1:10 scale chassis layout, optimizing weight distribution, component packaging, and torsional rigidity.
  • Bespoke Gearbox & Drivetrain: Designed, modeled, and manufactured a custom gearbox assembly built specifically for mounting integration, gear mesh efficiency, and durability under load.
  • Kinematic Suspension System: Developed a double-wishbone suspension setup, balancing camber recovery, roll center position, and damper mounting points to enhance high-speed cornering stability.
  • Iterative Aerodynamic & Track Testing: Conducted iterative manufacturing and physical testing runs to refine aero element performance, structural rigidity, and overall dynamic handling characteristics.
