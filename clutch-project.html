Clutch Tool Reproduction

Problem
A relative of mine, who is a motorcycle mechanic, was repairing a classic russian motorcycle for one of his clients. He needed an alignment tool for assembling the clutch plates, but due to the age of the bike and lack of standardization in early motorbike design, the tool was difficult to obtain. It would’ve cost him £60~ and taken at least a week to ship.

Solution
I took the clutch plates and some callipers, modelled the spline pattern in Fusion 360 and printed a replica tool all within an hour for about 30 pence in material. Saving him £60 and a week.

This was quite a simple case, but I hope you can see the utility of such an ability in a fast-paced environment. If you've ever tried to dissassemble an item but realized you needed a specialized tool to do so, you know how frustrating it can be. It takes time and money away from what should've otherwise been a simple job.
