SSGT Project

This is my first car design project with the intent to manufacture. Its currently designated as a "Single Seat Grand Tourer" (SSGT).

I've had a long-standing fascination with the idea of touring the country in a car of my own design. I had no intention of creating a money pit however, so I designed with reducing costs in mind. This is why I ended up with a single seater configuration, it allows me to compact the car greatly and forgo the additional cost of a passenger seat. It's not intended to be the ultimate low cost car, but I wanted the sort of "architecture" of the car to be low cost so that I could more comfortably spend money on higher quality components within budget. I'm going to be driving this regularly for long distance, so it does require decent quality.

As of 17th May, the car currently sits at the following stage:
- Package layout/Dimensions/Proportions are defined
- The suspension dynamics/kinematics have been defined
- Body design needs extra polish with regards to the greenhouse design, my A-pillars have turned out too thick for example, though the identity of the car will remain mostly as you see it.
